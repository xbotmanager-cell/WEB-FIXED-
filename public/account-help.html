👤 Account Help

Manage your De-King account and personal settings

Common Questions

How do I create an account?

Click "Register" at the top of the page. Enter your email address, create a password, and fill in your personal information. Verify your email by clicking the link we send you. Your account is now ready to use!

How do I reset my password?

Click "Login," then select "Forgot password?" Enter your registered email address. We'll send you a password reset link. Click it to create a new password. Make sure to store it securely.

How do I update my account information?

Log into your account, go to "Account Settings," and click "Edit Profile." You can update your name, email, phone number, address, and other personal information. Save your changes when done.

What is a seller account?

A seller account gives you access to additional features like creating listings, accessing the Seller Center, and selling items. You can upgrade your account from the Account Settings page.

How do I close my account?

Go to Account Settings, scroll to the bottom, and click "Close Account." Note: You cannot close an account with active listings or pending transactions. Please resolve these first.

How do I manage my personal data?

Visit Account Settings > Privacy & Security. Here you can view, download, or delete your personal data. We comply with GDPR and other data protection regulations.

How do I change my email address?

Log in, go to Account Settings > Contact Information. Click the email field and enter your new email. We'll send a verification link to confirm the change. Your old email will be replaced once verified.

Is my account secure?

We use 256-bit SSL encryption to protect your data. We recommend using a strong password, enabling two-factor authentication, and never sharing your login credentials. Review Account Settings > Security regularly.

💡 Account Security Tips

  • Create a strong password: Use at least 8 characters including numbers, letters, and symbols.
  • Enable two-factor authentication: Add an extra security layer with 2FA in Account Settings.
  • Keep email updated: Use a current email address for account recovery and notifications.
  • Don't share your password: De-King staff will never ask for your password.
  • Log out on shared devices: Always log out if using a public or shared computer.
  • Monitor your account: Review login activity and authorized devices regularly.
  • Verify links before clicking: Legitimate emails come from @deking.com addresses.
