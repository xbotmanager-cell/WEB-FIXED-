🛒 Buying Help

Everything you need to know about purchasing items on De-King

Common Questions

How do I search for items?

Use the search bar at the top of any page to find specific items. You can filter results by category, price, condition, and more. Type keywords or item numbers to narrow down your search.

How do I make a purchase?

Browse items, select the one you want, choose your quantity and options, then click "Buy Now" or "Place Bid" (for auctions). Add items to your cart, proceed to checkout, and complete payment. You'll receive an order confirmation via email.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), debit cards, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. All payments are secure and encrypted. Choose your preferred method at checkout.

Can I bid on auctions as a guest?

No, you must create an account to bid on auction items. However, you can purchase items listed as "Buy It Now" without logging in. Creating an account is free and takes just a few minutes.

How do I track my order?

Once your item ships, you'll receive a tracking number via email. Log into your account, go to "Purchase History," and click on your order to see real-time tracking information.

Can I cancel an order?

You can cancel most orders within 30 minutes of purchase if the seller hasn't shipped it yet. Visit your Purchase History, select the order, and click "Cancel Order." Some items may not be cancellable.

💡 Buying Tips

  • Check seller ratings: Always review seller feedback before purchasing. Look for 95%+ positive feedback.
  • Read item descriptions carefully: Pay attention to condition, size, color, and any defects mentioned.
  • Review photos: Zoom in on product photos to get a better look at the item's condition.
  • Check return policies: Understand the seller's return window and condition requirements.
  • Ask questions: Contact sellers before bidding if you need clarification on item details.
  • Use "Watch" feature: Add items to your watch list to track price changes and get notifications.
  • Set bid limits: Decide your maximum bid amount before participating in auctions to avoid overpaying.
