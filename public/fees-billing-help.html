💳 Fees & Billing

Understand our fees and billing information for buyers and sellers

Selling Fees

As a seller, you'll encounter these types of fees:

Fee Type Amount Description
Insertion Fee $0.30 - $4.80 Fee to list an item. Amount depends on starting price or reserve price.
Final Value Fee 12.9% of sale price Percentage of final sale price charged when item sells.
Featured Listing $3.95 - $79.95 Optional upgrade to promote your listing in search results.
Gallery Picture $0.15 per image Add multiple product photos to your listing.
Bold Title $2.00 Make your listing title stand out in search results.
Store Subscription $27.95/month Open an online store with customizable shop page.

Buyer Information

As a buyer, you pay for items and shipping. Payment methods include:

  • 💳 Credit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover)
  • 💳 Debit Cards
  • 🏪 PayPal
  • 📱 Apple Pay
  • 📱 Google Pay

No transaction fees for buyers! You only pay for the item and shipping.

Common Questions

Why am I being charged a fee?

Fees help us maintain our platform and keep the marketplace safe and secure. For sellers, fees cover listing and transaction costs. These fees help support buyer protection and seller tools.

Can I get a fee refund?

If you cancel a listing before it sells, insertion fees are non-refundable. If an item is withdrawn after receiving bids/offers, fees typically are not refunded. Final Value Fees are refunded if the sale is cancelled.

What if I accidentally paid twice?

Contact our billing support immediately with your order number and payment receipts. We'll investigate duplicate charges and process a refund within 5-7 business days if confirmed.

How do I pay my fees?

Fees are automatically charged to your registered payment method (usually the same card or account used for the transaction). You'll receive an invoice via email for your records.

Can I get an invoice for my purchases?

Yes! Log into your account, go to "Account Settings," and select "Invoices" or "Receipts." You can view and download invoices for all transactions dating back several years.

What are subscription fees?

Store subscriptions are optional monthly fees that give sellers access to a storefront, advanced analytics, and additional listing upgrades. You can cancel anytime in your account settings.

Are there different fee structures?

Yes, fees vary by category, location, and seller status. Some categories have different Final Value Fee percentages. Contact us for detailed fee information for your specific category.

How do I dispute a charge?

If you believe a charge is incorrect, go to "Account Settings" > "Billing" and click "Report a Problem." Provide details and supporting documentation. We'll review and respond within 5 business days.

💡 Money-Saving Tips

  • Plan your listings: Avoid listing errors that result in wasted insertion fees.
  • Use free upgrades: Many great features are free (good description, clear photos, etc.).
  • Batch listings: Create multiple listings in one session to save time and fees.
  • Monitor store subscriptions: Cancel if you're not actively selling.
  • Check fee estimates: Our calculator shows you estimated fees before listing.
  • Combine items: Bundle items to increase average order value vs. listing individually.
  • Use promotions wisely: Optional upgrades should focus on high-value items only.
