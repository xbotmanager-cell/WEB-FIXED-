Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about buying, redeeming, and using king Gift Cards. Expand any question to view the answer.

What can gift cards be used for?

king Gift Cards can be used to shop almost anything on our website. They are not valid for third-party gift cards, cash withdrawals, or items restricted by merchant policy.

Do I need an account to use a gift card?

Yes, you need an active account and a shipping address to redeem a gift card at checkout. You can create an account at any time if you don't already have one.

Does my gift card expire?

Most king Gift Cards do not expire and do not carry additional fees. Check the card terms for exceptions or promotional offers.

What happens if my order is less than the gift card amount?

Any remaining balance will stay on your account and can be used on future purchases. Select the gift card at checkout to apply the remaining balance.

Where can I get help if I have trouble redeeming my gift card?

If you experience trouble redeeming your gift card, contact our support team or review our help pages for step-by-step guidance.
