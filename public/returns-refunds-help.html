↩️ Returns & Refunds

Learn about our return policy and how to get refunds

How Returns Work

1

Check Return Eligibility

Items must be returned within the seller's return window (usually 14-30 days) in original condition.

2

Contact the Seller

Go to Purchase History, select the item, and click "Return or Refund." Follow the seller's instructions.

3

Ship the Item Back

Get a return shipping label, pack the item securely, and ship it back within 30 days.

4

Receive Your Refund

Once the seller confirms receipt, your refund will be processed within 5-7 business days.

Common Questions

Can I return an item?

Most items can be returned within 14-30 days if they're unused and in original condition. Some categories like electronics have specific conditions. Check the listing for the seller's return policy.

Who pays for return shipping?

The seller typically provides a prepaid shipping label for returns. If you opened a case for item not as described, shipping is usually free for you.

How long does a refund take?

After the seller confirms receipt of the returned item, your refund is usually processed within 5-7 business days. It may take an additional 3-5 days to appear in your account.

What if the seller won't accept my return?

If you believe the return is valid, you can open a case in our Resolution Center. We'll investigate and help you resolve the issue fairly.

Can I return an item if I changed my mind?

This depends on the seller's return policy. Some sellers accept returns for any reason within 30 days, while others only accept returns for defective items.

What about damaged or defective items?

If your item arrives damaged or defective, contact the seller immediately. Take photos of the damage and provide them as evidence. You may be eligible for a full refund without returning the item.

How do I track my return shipment?

Once you ship your return, you'll receive a tracking number. Use it to monitor your package's progress. Share the tracking info with the seller for faster processing.

What if my refund hasn't arrived?

Contact the seller to confirm they received your return. Check your bank or PayPal account for the refund. If you don't see it after 10 days, open a case in the Resolution Center.

💡 Return Tips

  • Read the return policy: Every seller has different terms. Always check before purchasing.
  • Act quickly: Start the return process as soon as you realize you want to return an item.
  • Keep proof: Save tracking numbers, receipts, and photos of the item's condition.
  • Pack securely: Use proper packaging materials to prevent damage during return shipping.
  • Document everything: Take photos of the item before shipping it back for your records.
  • Use tracking: Always ship returns with a tracking number for proof of delivery.
  • Communicate: Keep in contact with the seller throughout the return process.
