Selling FAQ

Answers to common questions about selling on De-King

Business selling Tips for listing FAQ My eBay
How do I list an item for sale? ▼
Click "Sell" in the navigation, fill out the listing form with item details, upload photos, set your price, and choose shipping options. Our AI-powered tools can help with pricing suggestions and descriptions. Your item will be live within minutes.
What are the listing fees? ▼
We charge an insertion fee of $0.30-$4.80 depending on category. When your item sells, we collect a Final Value Fee (typically 12.9% of the sale price). Optional features like featured listings, gallery pictures, and bold titles have separate fees. See our Fees & Billing page for complete details.
How long does a listing stay active? ▼
You can choose listing durations of 7, 14, or 30 days. After your selected duration ends, the listing expires automatically. You can relist items or renew existing listings to keep them active. Items that sell end immediately upon completion of the sale.
Can I edit my listing after posting? ▼
Yes! You can edit most listing details at any time, including title, description, price, and photos. Changes take effect immediately. If your item has received bids, you cannot change the price or quantity. Revisions help you respond to buyer inquiries and optimize your listing.
How do I get paid for items I sell? ▼
Payments are deposited to your bank account, PayPal, or other linked payment method within 1-3 business days after the buyer receives their item. We handle all payment processing securely with fraud detection and buyer protection built-in, so you don't need to process payments directly.
What if a buyer doesn't pay? ▼
If a buyer doesn't pay within 4 days, you can open an unpaid case. We'll send payment reminders automatically. After 7 days of non-payment, you can close the case and relist the item. Repeat non-payers can be blocked from bidding on your items in the future.
How should I handle item returns? ▼
If a buyer requests a return, communicate promptly and follow our return guidelines. Check item eligibility and condition upon receipt. Process refunds within 2 business days of receiving the returned item. Most returns must be initiated within 30 days of delivery. Building a reputation for fair returns increases buyer confidence.
What items am I not allowed to sell? ▼
Prohibited items include weapons, counterfeit goods, live animals, hazardous materials, and stolen property. Restricted items like electronics require verified seller status. Tobacco and alcohol may have age verification requirements. Review our complete prohibited and restricted items list before listing to ensure compliance.
How can I improve my seller rating? ▼
Provide accurate item descriptions, ship quickly and securely, communicate professionally with buyers, and resolve issues fairly. High positive ratings lead to increased visibility and buyer trust. Aim for 4.8+ stars to become a "Top Rated" seller with eligibility for promotions and discounts.
Can I offer promotions or discounts? ▼
Yes! You can create promotional offers, offer bulk discounts, or run sales on your items. De-King provides promotional tools to help you reach more buyers. Strategic discounts on slow-moving items can increase sales velocity and generate positive reviews from satisfied customers.
What shipping options should I offer? ▼
Offer multiple shipping options: standard 5-7 day shipping, expedited 2-3 day shipping, and next-day options for premium buyers. Consider offering free shipping on competitive pricing to attract more bidders. Use flat-rate shipping when it benefits you or calculated shipping for accurate costs.
