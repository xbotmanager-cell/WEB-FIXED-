🏷️ Selling Help

Get started as a seller and maximize your sales on De-King

Common Questions

How do I start selling?

First, create a De-King account or log in. Then visit the Seller Center and click "Start Selling." You'll need to provide basic information and agree to our seller terms. After that, you can create your first listing.

What items can I sell?

You can sell most items including electronics, fashion, collectibles, home goods, and more. However, some items are restricted (hazardous materials, counterfeit goods, etc.). Review our restricted items policy before listing.

How do I create a listing?

Go to Seller Center, click "Create Listing," select your item category, add photos, write a description, set your price or starting bid, and choose auction or fixed-price format. Review everything and publish your listing.

What are the selling fees?

Insertion fees range from $0.30-$4.80 depending on item value. Final Value Fees are typically 12.9% of the sale price. Premium services like featured listings have additional costs. See our Fees page for detailed information.

How do I get paid?

After a buyer completes payment, funds are deposited to your registered bank account or PayPal within 1-3 business days. You can track payments in your Seller Center dashboard.

How do I handle disputes?

If a buyer opens a case, you'll receive a notification. You can respond with evidence (tracking, photos, etc.) to resolve the issue. De-King's Resolution Center helps mediate disputes fairly.

💡 Seller Tips

  • Use high-quality photos: Take clear, well-lit photos from multiple angles. Use our photo editing tools.
  • Write detailed descriptions: Include size, condition, color, defects, and all relevant details. Honest descriptions reduce returns.
  • Price competitively: Research similar items to set competitive prices and attract buyers.
  • Ship quickly: Process orders within 1-2 business days to maintain good seller ratings.
  • Offer multiple shipping options: Let buyers choose between standard and expedited shipping.
  • Build positive feedback: Communicate promptly, ship carefully, and handle issues professionally.
  • Use keywords strategically: Include relevant search terms in your title and description.
