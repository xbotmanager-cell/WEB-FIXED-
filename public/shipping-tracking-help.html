Common Questions

How do I track my order?

Log into your account, go to "Purchase History," select your order, and click "Track Package." You'll see real-time updates from pickup to delivery. You can also use the tracking number emailed to you.

How long does shipping take?

Delivery times depend on your selected shipping method and location. Standard shipping typically takes 5-7 business days. Check the estimated delivery date shown in your order details.

What is the shipping cost?

Shipping costs vary based on item weight, destination, and selected method. You'll see the total shipping cost before completing checkout. Some items may qualify for free shipping.

Can I change my shipping address?

You can change your address during checkout before finalizing payment. After purchase, contact us immediately if the item hasn't shipped yet. Changes may not be possible once shipping has started.

What if my package is delayed?

If your package is delayed beyond the estimated delivery date, check the tracking for updates. Contact the carrier for status. If significantly delayed, contact the seller or our support team.

How do I report a missing package?

Check with neighbors or look for delivery notifications. Wait 24-48 hours for delivery attempts. Then contact the seller or our support team with your tracking number to file a claim.

What if my package arrives damaged?

Document the damage with photos immediately. Contact the seller within 48 hours of delivery. Provide photos and your tracking number. You may be eligible for a replacement or full refund.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes! We ship to many countries worldwide. International shipping times vary by destination (typically 2-4 weeks). Additional customs fees may apply based on local regulations.

💡 Shipping Tips

  • Choose appropriate shipping: Balance cost and delivery speed based on your needs.
  • Add insurance: For valuable items, consider adding shipping insurance for protection.
  • Track your package: Monitor tracking regularly to know when to expect delivery.
  • Be home for delivery: Be available during the estimated delivery window.
  • Save tracking info: Keep tracking numbers for your records (usually valid for 1 year).
  • Check signatures: High-value items may require a signature at delivery.
  • Report issues promptly: If something seems wrong, report it quickly to preserve evidence.
